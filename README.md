For one person watching on one screen at a time, the **1-connection plan** is the obvious starting point. There is little reason to pay for extra connections if the TV in the living room is doing all the work.

## Is IPTV legal?

IPTV itself is simply a way of delivering television content over the internet. The important issue is whether a particular provider has the necessary rights or licenses for the content it distributes. That question is separate from the technology itself.

For that reason, anyone comparing an IPTV service should look at both the technical details and the provider's own information about the content it offers.
